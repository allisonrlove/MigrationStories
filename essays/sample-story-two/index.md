## How communities take root

This sample paragraph connects one family's story to the wider history of migration. It might explain how newcomers often settled near others from the same region, how chain migration brought relatives and friends, and how shared institutions such as religious centers, mutual aid groups, and local businesses helped people adjust. It might also discuss the obstacles families met, including discrimination, language barriers, and restrictive laws. A real essay draws on class readings and other secondary sources here and cites them in Chicago style, in about 150 to 250 words.
