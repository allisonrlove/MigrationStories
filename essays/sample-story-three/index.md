## Conflict, displacement, and resettlement

This sample paragraph connects one family's story to the wider history of migration. It might explain the political events that forced people to leave, the journeys that followed, and the policies that governed who could be admitted and resettled. It might also discuss the role of faith groups, relief organizations, and local sponsors in helping newcomers begin again. ## The journey, an object, and a sense of home

This sample paragraph describes the journey as a whole, including the decision to leave, the travel, and the first years of resettlement. It would introduce an object that the family kept, such as a photograph, a document, or a keepsake, and explain what it means to them. It would reflect on identity and place: what it means to belong in two places at once, and how younger generations carry the story forward. Students draw directly on the oral history and cite it.
